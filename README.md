## Analysis & Insights

# Recruitment Efficiency
Recruiters are performing well above expectations. The average time to hire is **21 days**, which is **14 days faster** than the 35‑day benchmark. This shows the team is moving candidates through the process quickly without sacrificing quality
# Departmental Trends
Most departments show a healthy balance between applications and offers. However, **Sales and IT** stand out with more medium‑priority roles, which could signal heavier workloads or more complex hiring needs. Finance, HR, Marketing, and Operations remain steady and balanced.
# Pipeline Health
The funnel stages are consistent: Screening (23), Round 1 (22), Technical (22), and Offered (22). The minimal drop‑off between stages suggests strong candidate filtering and effective pipeline management.
# Sourcing Channels
All successful offers came from **LinkedIn and Internal Referrals**, split evenly at 50% each. These channels are clearly reliable, but the reliance on just two sources may limit reach. Exploring additional channels could diversify the talent pool.
# Cost Analysis
The **average cost per hire is $2.39K**, stable across January and February 2026. While this is efficient, more months of data will help confirm whether this stability holds over time.
---
# Recommendations
- **Boost conversion rate:** At 22%, there’s room to improve the percentage of applicants who receive offers.  
- **Support Sales & IT:** These departments may need extra recruiter focus due to higher medium‑priority workloads.  
- **Diversify sourcing:** Add new channels (job boards, campus programs, social platforms) to reduce dependency on LinkedIn/referrals.  
- **Expand dataset**:** Track cost per hire over more months to identify long‑term trends and forecast future spend.
---
## Conclusion
This dashboard highlights how HR teams can monitor recruiter efficiency, departmental workload, and sourcing balance in one view. The data shows recruiters are faster than target, costs are under control, and sourcing is effective — but there are clear opportunities to improve conversion rates and broaden recruitment channels. Overall, it’s a strong foundation for data‑driven HR decision‑making.
